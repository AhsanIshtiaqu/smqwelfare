Let Us Come Together To Make a Difference

Sheraz Mehmood Qureshi's enduring legacy and with a commitment to extending his impactful efforts, the Sheraz Mehmood Qureshi Welfare Organization was established. Inspired by his values, the organization is dedicated to fostering empowerment among individuals and communities through compassionate and enduring initiatives. 


Our mission extends beyond merely offering assistance to the community; we strive to equip individuals with the tools and resources necessary to cultivate improved livelihoods for themselves and their families. This encompasses a range of developmental projects and capacity-building programs tailored to empower communities.


Mission

To empower and improve the lives of individuals and communities in need through compassionate and sustainable programs and services

Vision

Our vision is a world where every individual has access to basic needs and opportunities for personal and community development. We strive to empower individuals and communities through education, health, and economic development.
